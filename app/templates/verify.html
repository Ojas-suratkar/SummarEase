What this check establishes

That these records have not been altered, reordered, or had entries added or removed since they were sealed. Each record commits to the one before it, so a change anywhere breaks everything after it.

What it does not

That the contents are true. A sealed statement is still a statement.

That the records were made when they claim — unless the fingerprint was published somewhere independent at the time. Without that, the dates are this system's own assertion. Publishing the seal is the step that removes the doubt, and it takes a minute.

Checking this without trusting us

The exported pack contains verify.py, which repeats this check using only the Python standard library. It reads the files in the pack, recomputes every fingerprint, and names any record that does not match. It is short enough to read before running.

That matters because the other side in a dispute has no reason to trust software you chose. They do not have to.
