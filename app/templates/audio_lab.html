Audio fingerprints

Drop in a recording and this works out whether you've heard it before, and where the same audio repeats inside it. It's the same technique music-identification apps use: turn the sound into a spectrogram, find the loudest peaks, and hash the pattern between them. Unlike comparing files byte for byte, it still matches after re-encoding, volume changes and background noise.
