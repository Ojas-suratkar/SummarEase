Unlock

Unlocking happens on this device. There is nothing to check the passphrase against until an item decrypts, so the first wrong passphrase shows up as an item that will not open.

If you forget this passphrase, the notes are gone.

There is no reset link and no recovery process. We do not hold the passphrase, a copy of it, or anything derived from it. Support cannot help. That is the same property that stops us handing your notes to anyone else.

Write the passphrase down and keep it somewhere safe, or store it in a password manager. Use a passphrase you do not use anywhere else.

How it works
  • Your passphrase is stretched into a key with PBKDF2-SHA256, 600,000 iterations, using a random 16-byte salt for each item.
  • Each note is encrypted with AES-256-GCM and a fresh random 12-byte IV.
  • The key is marked non-extractable, so no script on this page can read its bytes.
  • The passphrase stays in memory on this device. It is not stored and not sent.
  • The vault locks itself after 10 minutes of inactivity and whenever you leave the tab.
